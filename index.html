    05 / FAQ

    Good questions

    Anything else on your mind? Ask our assistant for an instant answer, or message the team directly.

    DM us
    Who do you work with?

    Shops and product businesses of every size, wherever they are. We work online, so distance is never a problem.

    Do you build websites?

    Yes. For R500 once-off we design and build a website for your shop or product that looks great on phones and computers. You can get it on its own or together with any ad plan.

    How do I pay?

    Cash or card, whichever suits you.

    Can I change plans later?

    Yes. Message us any time and we will move you to a different plan.

    What is the difference between monthly and yearly?

    Same plan, same work. Paying yearly gets you two months free compared to paying month by month.

    How do I get started?

    Pick a plan or the website build and send it to us by email or WhatsApp in one tap. You can also DM us on Instagram or TikTok. We reply with the next steps.
